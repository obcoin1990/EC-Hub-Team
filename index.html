Partner FAQ

How fast are commissions paid?

Commissions are paid within 2 hours.

Is there a limit on sub-IB levels?

No. The multi-level structure is unlimited, and you earn your agreed share on your sub-IBs' clients.

How is my rebate set?

Your BDM agrees a rebate per lot for each account group (for example STD or STD-PX) and asset class. You can then split it with your sub-IBs.

What can I use to promote EC Markets?

Use the approved materials and posts your BDM provides, and always include the risk warning. Do not promise profits or returns.

Earnings depend on your clients' trading activity and are not guaranteed. Partner terms are set in your partner agreement. CFD trading is high risk and losses may exceed deposits.

Compliance · Category 5 licence

We promote and introduce. Nothing more.

Every call, message and post must stay inside our licensed scope.

You can
  • Identify the company and your role clearly on every call
  • Explain products, accounts, platforms and the partner programme
  • Use only approved scripts, materials, disclosures and disclaimers
  • Get Compliance approval before any campaign, event or public post
  • Record every material interaction, introduction and escalation
  • Escalate requests for advice, trades, account access or funds to your manager and Compliance
You must never
  • Give investment advice or recommend a trading strategy
  • Guarantee, promise or predict returns, or make unverified claims
  • Pressure a client to invest
  • Ask for or keep a client's password, OTP or portal login
  • Open a client's portal, place or manage trades
  • Give instructions on deposits, withdrawals or client funds
Calls & messaging

Every client conversation goes through company channels

Approved tools

Company SIM, 3CX extension, company email, CRM and company WhatsApp. Access is granted after training and manager approval.

UAE telemarketing rules

Approved calling practices, consent and do-not-call controls, identification and a recording notice on every call.

Security

Keep 3CX logins, SIMs, passwords and OTPs private. Report loss, SIM-swap or phishing immediately to your manager, IT and Compliance.

Zero tolerance
  • Moving a client to your personal number or personal WhatsApp
  • Deleting, hiding or altering call logs, chats or recordings
  • Screenshotting, exporting or forwarding client data to personal accounts
  • Using company tools for side business or private commissions

Bypassing communication monitoring is listed as gross misconduct in the Employee Handbook.

Report suspected fraud, money laundering, complaints or unusual client behaviour to Compliance / MLRO, and never tip off the client.
